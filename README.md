# Nautilus

Nautilus is a family of three bodies for working with humans and agents: a framework that gives the
work a form, an app where the work happens, and a protocol that lets both recognize the same
grammar.

Like the nautilus, it grows by adding chambers without losing its form.

## The family

- **[Nautilus Agentic Framework](https://github.com/mirror-mind-ai/nautilus-framework)**: the
  grammar. Participants, human and agentic, work in Journeys, formulate Missions, produce
  Deliveries, recognize Realizations through Evidence and integrate what they learn.
- **[Nautilus App](https://github.com/mirror-mind-ai/nautilus-app)**: a macOS app, published as
  *Nautilus for Mirror*, for working in Journeys with an agent that knows your
  [Mirror](https://github.com/mirror-mind-ai/mirror). Site:
  [nautilus.mirrormind.sh](https://nautilus.mirrormind.sh).
- **[Nautilus Agentic Protocol](https://github.com/mirror-mind-ai/nautilus-protocol)**: the
  portable, executable form of the grammar: schemas, documents and conformance tests that say
  "this is Nautilus, in this version", without depending on any one app.

This repository only presents the family. Each body has its own repository, history and releases.
