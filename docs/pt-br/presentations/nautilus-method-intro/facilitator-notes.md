# Nautilus Method Intro, facilitator notes

## Intent

This deck introduces Nautilus Agentic Method as a living grammar, not as a finished framework. The goal is to make the method understandable to outsiders while preserving its experimental nature.

Audience should leave with three recognitions:

- Nautilus is about realization with coherence, not task management.
- The method is part of a four-body family: Method, Protocol, Mirror Extension and Harness.
- The current version is intentionally minimal and grows by semantic chambers.

## Suggested pacing

Use 20 to 30 minutes for a first presentation.

The strongest rhythm is contemplative rather than explanatory. Let the nautilus image do conceptual work before entering vocabulary. Avoid trying to prove the whole system. The deck should make people feel the grammar before they inspect it.

## Narrative arc

Open with the image of growth by chambers. The audience does not need the whole ontology at the beginning. They need to feel the contrast between expansion and loss of form.

Then name the current failure mode of agentic work: more tasks, more outputs, more acceleration, but weak continuity between intention, evidence and learning.

Only after that introduce Nautilus as a grammar. The word grammar matters because it suggests shared structure without reducing the work to a tool.

When presenting the four bodies, keep the distinction clean. Method speaks human meaning. Protocol speaks formal correspondence. Mirror Extension adapts the grammar to Mirror. Harness becomes the dedicated experience.

The central vocabulary should be presented as a surface, not as a full ontology. The method is experimental. Its discipline is to add one chamber at a time.

## Emphasis by slide

### Nautilus Agentic Method

Name the promise in one sentence: grow in realization without losing coherence.

### A intuição fundadora

Do not over-explain the biological metaphor. The important move is from natural growth to semantic growth.

### O problema que Nautilus enfrenta

Keep this grounded. People already feel tool sprawl, task sprawl and agent acceleration. Nautilus enters as a way to preserve form inside that acceleration.

### O que é Nautilus

Use this as the first formal definition. If needed, say: a grammar is not yet a product, but it can later be practiced through a product.

### A família Nautilus

Stress that these bodies must correspond. If Method says one thing and Protocol encodes another, the family loses form.

### O papel do Method

This slide protects the method from becoming implementation-first. Method owns meaning before mechanics.

### A superfície mínima

Read the concepts slowly. Do not define all at once. The following slides unfold the most important ones.

### Jornada

Position Jornada as continuity. It is broader than project and more practical than vague life theme.

### Participante

Explain why Participante won over Nauta as a canonic term. Nautical language can enrich experience, but the method needs a stable neutral ontology.

### Missão

Make clear that formulation is a real phase. Naming intention before execution is part of the work.

### O Teste de Inteireza da Missão

This is one of the most teachable parts of the method. It converts philosophy into a practical check.

### Entrega, Evidência, Realização

Distinguish completion from realization. This is the heart of the method's critique of ordinary task systems.

### Integração

Let this slide breathe. Integration is where the spiral metaphor becomes operational.

### Como Nautilus cresce

Use this to explain why the current version is small. Minimality is method discipline, not incompleteness by accident.

### O que Nautilus não é

This slide prevents premature categorization. People may try to fit Nautilus into project management, productivity or automation. Accept the proximity, then distinguish the method.

### Síntese

This can be the closing definition.

### Próxima câmara

End by inviting pressure from real cases. The method should grow from use, not from abstract completeness.

## Adaptation notes

For a technical audience, emphasize correspondence across the four bodies and the YAML Mission fixture.

For a leadership or learning audience, emphasize Jornada, Realização and Integração.

For people skeptical of agents, emphasize that Nautilus does not erase human authority. It gives humans and agents a shared grammar while preserving their difference.

For people excited by agents, emphasize that acceleration without evidence and integration creates fragile growth.

## Open questions to preserve

- When does a Mission become ready to execute?
- What kind of Evidence is proportional to each kind of Delivery?
- How does Integration become visible without becoming bureaucratic?
- How should Role and Authority enter without overloading the first surface?
