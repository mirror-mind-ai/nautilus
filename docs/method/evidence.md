# Evidence

Evidence is the proportional trace that sustains the recognition that [value](value-generation.md) was made available through a [Realization](realization.md). It is the mechanism of the **Grounded Progress** principle.

**A Journey should only recognize progress when there is a trustworthy trace that value was made available, transformed, or learned in the Human Territory.**

It allows [Participants](participant.md) to trust that something moved in the [Human Territory](human-territory.md), instead of depending only on the subjective feeling of progress.

## Why the concept exists

Nautilus distinguishes effort, production, Value Generation, Realization, and Impact.

For this distinction not to remain abstract, the Journey needs evidence. Evidence shows what value became available, why progress can be recognized, and how this recognition can sustain the next movement.

## Evidence as high-level verification

Evidence of Realization plays, at the highest level of value generation, a role analogous to automated tests, reviews, or quality and integrity checks at lower levels.

At the technical level, an automated test helps trust that a part of the system behaves as expected. A review helps perceive whether something preserves quality. An integrity check helps know whether a structure remains consistent.

At the Journey level, Evidence helps trust that effort became recognizable Realization. It verifies whether the movement made something real and available in the Human Territory: a sustained decision, an accessible Delivery, an observable change, a new capacity, integrated learning.

The analogy does not mean every Evidence must be automated or formal. It means Realization also needs proportional verifiability.

## Proportionality

Evidence should be proportional to the type of Realization.

An implementation may ask for tests, smoke checks, or behavioral observation. An article may ask for publication and accessibility. A book may ask for publication, distribution, listing, or availability for purchase or reading. A decision may ask for record, commitment, agenda change, or consequent action.

Too much Evidence becomes bureaucracy. Too little weakens recognition.

## Evidence of production, Realization, Impact, and Value

Not every evidence of production is Evidence of Realization, and not every sign of [Impact](impact.md) should be required to recognize Realization.

A saved file proves that something was produced. It does not prove, by itself, that the Journey realized what mattered. A published article or book can prove that the Delivery was made available in the Human Territory. Reading, resonance, citations, sales, recommendations, or transformation are signs of Impact after availability. A passing test proves technical behavior. It does not prove, by itself, that the delivery became available to the expected Human Territory.

Value lenses affect how later evidence is interpreted. Contact and response signs may support the pragmatic lens when they show that someone received, chose, used, paid, or continued. Field-effect signs may support the integrative lens when they show increased capacity, clarity, trust, relationship, coherence, or continuity in the Human Territory.

Nautilus preserves these differences.

## Role of the agentic Participant

The agentic [Participant](participant.md) should help the human Participant collect, refine, and interpret Evidence proportional to the Realization being sought.

This help may appear as suggesting sources, preparing verification instruments, reading metrics, comparing signals, organizing records, or formulating a simple confirmation route. The agent does not replace human recognition of Realization, but can make that recognition better sustained.

If the Mission is to publish an article, for example, the agent may first help distinguish Evidence of Realization from Impact signals. Evidence of Realization may include the article being published, accessible, correctly linked, and announced to the intended field. Impact signals may include reading and view metrics, comments, responses, shares, citations, or use in a decision.

## Practical criterion

Evidence is good when it helps Participants answer:

- which value was made available;
- which Realization made it available;
- what became available or different in the Human Territory;
- what trace sustains that availability or difference;
- whether the Evidence is proportional to the Realization;
- which later signals belong to Impact;
- which value lens those signals support;
- whether it helps the next session continue with more confidence.

## Synthesis

Evidence is the proportional verification of Realization. At the high level of the Journey, it makes progress reliable, just as tests and checks make work reliable at lower levels.
