# Journey

A Journey is the field of growth where human and agentic [Participants](participant.md) conduct realization over time.

It is not merely a project, a goal, or a task list. A project tends to be defined by scope and delivery. A goal tends to be defined by a desired result. A task list tends to be defined by pending actions. The Journey may include these elements when necessary, but its main function is to preserve continuity, direction, memory, and learning.

## Why Journey exists

Nautilus needs a concept capable of sustaining work that does not end in a single delivery.

When humans and agents work together, many things can happen: conversations, decisions, documents, experiments, revisions, detours, discoveries, and partial deliveries. Without a Journey, these movements scatter. With a Journey, they can be recognized as part of a continuous crossing.

The Journey gives ground for asking: what are we crossing, what has already moved, what have we learned, and what new capacity is available for the next turn?

## What a Journey preserves

A Journey preserves:

- direction, so work does not become loose activity;
- memory, so learning is not lost among conversations and artifacts;
- coherence, so new chambers expand form without breaking it;
- continuity, so each [Realization](realization.md) can increase the capacity to realize the next one.

## Journey and Mission

A Journey hosts Missions.

The [Mission](mission.md) names a specific movement inside the Journey. The Journey preserves the larger field in which that movement gains meaning.

A Journey may have many Missions. Some will be retrospective, helping recognize what already happened. Others will be prospective, giving form to a movement before execution.

## Journey and Realization

[Realization](realization.md) happens inside a Journey.

This matters because an isolated Realization can become an episode. When it is integrated into the Journey, it increases future capacity. The Journey does not merely store what was done; it matures with what was recognized, evidenced, and integrated.

## Journey and agents

In Nautilus, a Journey may be crossed by human and agentic Participants.

This does not make humans and agents equivalent. The common grammar allows collaboration, but differences of nature, role, and authority remain important. The current version recognizes this distinction, but does not yet formalize Role and Authority.

## Practical criterion

A Journey is well named when it helps Participants answer:

- what field of growth is being crossed;
- why this field matters now;
- what continuity needs to be preserved;
- which previous Realizations have already increased current capacity;
- what next movement can be born without losing coherence.

## Boundary of the current version

In version `0.1.0-experimental`, Journey is a founding concept of the method language. There is not yet a complete formal structure for states, roles, authority, persistence, or the operational cycle of the Journey.
