# Delivery

Delivery is what a Mission produces, transforms, or makes available in the Journey.

It may be an artifact, decision, change of state, publication, implementation, structured conversation, adjusted practice, or any recognizable result that gives form to work.

## Why the concept exists

Nautilus distinguishes effort, Delivery, and Realization.

Effort is the energy invested. Delivery is the produced form or made-available change. [Realization](realization.md) is the recognition that this form became available in the [Human Territory](human-territory.md), was sustained by [Evidence](evidence.md), and can be integrated into the Journey.

A Delivery may exist without Realization. A draft article saved locally is a Delivery, but not yet a public Realization. A published and accessible article can be Realization when its availability in the expected Human Territory is evidenced. Reading, use, resonance, citation, or market response belong to [Impact](impact.md).

## Possible Delivery types

A Delivery may appear as:

- document;
- code;
- publication;
- decision;
- plan;
- synthesis;
- recorded conversation;
- operational change;
- stabilized practice;
- organized evidence;
- new available capacity.

The list is not closed. The main criterion is that something gained enough form to be recognized, used, verified, or integrated.

## Delivery and Evidence

Every Delivery must be able to point to proportional Evidence when used to recognize Realization.

The file proves that something was written. Publication and accessibility prove something was made available. Reading, comment, citation, sale, use, or consequent decision are Impact signals that may feed later Integration.

## Delivery and Realization

Delivery answers: what gained form?

Realization answers: what became available or different in the Human Territory?

This distinction protects the method from confusing production with progress. A Journey may generate many Deliveries and still not increase its capacity to realize.

## Practical criterion

A Delivery is well named when Participants can answer:

- what was produced, transformed, or made available;
- which Mission this Delivery answers;
- where this Delivery exists or can be observed;
- what Evidence can sustain its recognition;
- what it makes available in the Human Territory;
- what Impact it hopes to invite without pretending to control it.

## Boundary of the current version

In version `0.1.0-experimental`, Delivery is a founding concept of the method language, but its types, states, and formal criteria will be stabilized in future chambers.
