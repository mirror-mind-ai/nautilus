# Impact

Impact is what happens after realized [value](value-generation.md) becomes available in the [Human Territory](human-territory.md).

It may appear as reading, use, adoption, response, sale, citation, transformation, behavioral change, or any other qualitative or quantitative effect produced by what was realized.

## Why the concept exists

Nautilus needs to distinguish what the Journey can conduct from what it can only observe, influence, and learn from.

The method is centered on Value Generation. It is disciplined by Realization because Realization names the part of the movement that Participants can conduct with more agency: giving form to the work, producing or making available a Delivery, gathering proportional Evidence, recognizing that something now exists in the Human Territory, and integrating what was learned.

Impact comes next. It depends on the world receiving, using, interpreting, ignoring, sharing, resisting, or transforming what became available. Participants can design for Impact, invite it, measure it, and learn from it, but they do not control it.

## Value Generation, Realization, and Impact

Publishing a book is Realization when the book has gained form, crossed the Journey, and became available for consumption in the Human Territory.

Having readers is Impact.

The number of readers is Impact.

The way readers respond, change, cite, recommend, criticize, or ignore the book is Impact.

The same distinction applies to an article, a product, a class, a decision, a tool, or a public artifact. Value Generation is the larger purpose. Making value real and available belongs to Realization. What the world does with it belongs to Impact.

## Boundary of control

A Journey can control, or at least directly conduct, many conditions of Realization:

- whether the Mission is well formulated;
- whether the Delivery exists;
- whether it is accessible to the intended Human Territory;
- whether there is Evidence that it was made available;
- whether learning was integrated for the next movement.

A Journey cannot control Impact in the same way. It cannot guarantee readership, adoption, resonance, transformation, or market response.

This boundary protects the method from confusing responsibility with omnipotence. Nautilus asks Participants to realize what is theirs to realize, then observe Impact without pretending it was fully under their control.

## Measuring Impact

Impact can be assessed through quantitative and qualitative means.

Quantitative signs may include counts, views, sales, conversions, downloads, citations, completion rates, response rates, retention, or frequency of use.

Qualitative signs may include testimonials, conversations, decisions influenced, changes in practice, interpretation, resonance, criticism, stories of use, or observed transformation.

Both kinds of signs can become [Evidence](evidence.md) for later learning, but they should not be confused with the Evidence that sustains the original Realization.

## Relationship with Integration

Impact should feed [Integration](integration.md) when it teaches the Journey something useful.

A book with few readers may still be a valid Realization, but its limited Impact can teach something about distribution, audience, language, timing, or positioning. A public artifact with surprising use may reveal a stronger field of demand than expected.

Impact does not retroactively erase Realization. It informs the next spiral turn.

## Practical criterion

To distinguish Realization from Impact, ask:

- what was made real and available in the Human Territory?
- what Evidence sustains that availability?
- what happened after availability?
- which effects were within the Journey's control?
- which effects depended on reception by the world?
- what can be learned from that reception?

## Synthesis

Value Generation is the method's primary field.

Realization makes value real, available, evidenced, and integrated in the Human Territory.

Impact is what comes after: the world's response to what was realized. It can be measured and learned from, but not controlled in the same way.
