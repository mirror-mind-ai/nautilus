# Grammar

The current Nautilus grammar is a minimal surface of value generation and the concept page for the **Form in Service of Realization** principle.

**Each form in the method should help value become realized in the Human Territory without imposing more complexity than the Journey needs to carry.**

It organizes ten founding concepts: [Value Generation](value-generation.md), [Human Territory](human-territory.md), [Journey](journey.md), [Participant](participant.md), [Mission](mission.md), [Delivery](delivery.md), [Evidence](evidence.md), [Realization](realization.md), [Impact](impact.md), and [Integration](integration.md).

## Value Generation

[Value Generation](value-generation.md) is the central purpose of Nautilus.

It answers: what value is being generated, for whom, and through which Realization?

## Human Territory

[Human Territory](human-territory.md) is the field where value must become available or make a difference in order for Realization to be recognized as human or human-agentic.

It comes before the Journey because it defines where the method must touch the lived world: decisions, relationships, responsibilities, consequences, learning, and the capacity to continue.

## Journey

A [Journey](journey.md) is the field of growth.

It hosts continuity, memory, and direction. A Journey may contain many Missions, but it is not reducible to a task list or closed project. It preserves the larger question that gives meaning to the work.

A Journey answers:

- what field is being crossed;
- what continuity must be preserved;
- what kind of growth is at stake.

## Participant

A [Participant](participant.md) is a presence that participates in the Journey.

A Participant may have human or agentic nature. The term preserves a shared grammar without erasing differences of authority, experience, responsibility, and capacity.

The current version recognizes Participants, but does not yet define Role and Authority.

## Mission

A Mission is a unit of value generation through Realization in conduct.

It gives form to the movement before execution and carries the responsibility frame that distinguishes it from a task. A task executes inside an assumed frame. A Mission makes the frame readable: value, purpose, Participants, authority, proportional scope, possible Delivery, Evidence, and Integration.

In the current slice, Mission is formulative. It does not yet execute, assign full roles, produce delivery, collect evidence, mark realization, or integrate learning.

A minimal Mission has `id`, `title`, `purpose`, and `status`.

## Delivery

A [Delivery](delivery.md) is what the Mission produces, transforms, or makes available as a candidate carrier of value.

It may be an artifact, change, document, decision, implementation, preparation, structuring conversation, or any recognizable result. Delivery is not yet fully formalized in the current version, but it already appears as a necessary next concept of the grammar.

## Evidence

[Evidence](evidence.md) is the trace that sustains the recognition that value was made available through a Realization.

It answers: how do we know that something happened?

Evidence must be proportional to the Delivery. A document may have the file itself as evidence. A conversation may have minutes, decision, memory, or later observable change. An implementation may have tests, demonstration, or verifiable behavior.

## Realization

[Realization](realization.md) is the recognition that an intention crossed the field, touched the [Human Territory](human-territory.md), and made something available or different there.

Mission and Realization are different moments of one movement. Mission is Realization in conduct, before recognition. Realization is the recognition that value became available, sustained by Evidence.

Realization is not synonymous with completed task. A task may end without Realization when it does not preserve meaning, evidence, availability, or learning. A Realization may include a shift in understanding, stabilization of language, publication, availability, or change of state, as long as there is proportional Evidence and recognizable difference in the Human Territory.

## Impact

[Impact](impact.md) is what happens after a Realization becomes available in the Human Territory.

It may include reading, use, adoption, sale, citation, response, or transformation. Impact can be assessed and learned from, but is not controlled in the same way as Realization.

## Integration

[Integration](integration.md) is the gesture that preserves learning in the next growth.

It prevents Realization from becoming an isolated episode. Integration may update memory, adjust language, change a practice, record a decision, create a pattern, or modify the next Mission.

## Minimal relation

The founding relation can be read as:

```text
Value Generation gives direction.
Human Territory hosts Journeys.
Journeys are crossed by Participants.
Participants formulate Missions.
Missions orient tasks and Deliveries.
Tasks may serve one or more Missions.
Deliveries produce Evidence.
Evidence sustains Realizations.
Realizations may generate Impact.
Impact informs Integration.
Integration preserves form for the next chamber.
The next chamber returns to the Human Territory with more capacity to realize.
```

## Boundary of the current version

Version `0.1.0-experimental` stabilizes language, not the complete machine.

Execution, roles, authority, persistence, operational states, and the full Delivery cycle remain future chambers.
