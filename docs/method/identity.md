# Identity

## Name

**Nautilus Agentic Method** is the name of the conceptual and pedagogical layer of the [Nautilus Family](family.md), oriented to understanding and practicing the method.

**Nautilus** names the family and also the dedicated application experience.

## Version

```yaml
method: Nautilus Agentic Method
method_version: 0.1.0-experimental
grammar_status: experimental
```

This version is experimental because it does not yet intend to stabilize the complete ontology. It stabilizes a first surface of language to allow reading, experimentation, and common recognition among the components of the Nautilus Family.

## Nominal family

The [Nautilus Family](family.md) has four bodies:

| Body | Name | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Method | Nautilus Agentic Method | Human, conceptual, and pedagogical language |
| Protocol | Nautilus Agentic Protocol | Formal, portable, and verifiable declaration of the grammar |
| Mirror Extension | Nautilus Mirror Extension | Adapter and reference implementation in Mirror Mind |
| Harness | Nautilus Harness | Dedicated application for practicing Nautilus |

The family exists so that the same grammar can be understood by humans, declared formally, adapted to Mirror, and practiced in its own experience.

## Recognition fields

The first vertical identity expects all bodies to recognize these minimal fields:

```yaml
name: Nautilus
method_version: 0.1.0-experimental
protocol_version: 0.1.0
schema_version: 0.1.0
grammar_status: experimental
compatibility: compatible
```

These fields do not make the bodies identical. They preserve recognizability.

## Naming decisions

[`Participant`](participant.md) is the canonical neutral term for whoever participates in the grammar. A Participant may have human or agentic nature.

`Nauta` is not a canonical category of the method in this version.

Terms such as Crew, Crew member, or Navigator may inspire experiential language in the Harness, but do not replace the ontology. The nautical metaphor orients the method's sensitivity. It should not colonize every concept.

## Boundary

This identity does not define execution, persistence, roles, authority, complete [Evidence](evidence.md), or operational [Integration](integration.md). These elements appear as horizon of the grammar, but enter only when a future slice can sustain them coherently across the four bodies.
