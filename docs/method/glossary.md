# Glossary

## Nautilus

Name of the family and dedicated experience. The nautilus image sustains the principle of growth through coherent chambers.

## Nautilus Agentic Method

Conceptual and pedagogical layer of the [Nautilus Family](family.md), oriented to understanding and practicing the method. It gives language, meaning, and criteria to practice.

## Nautilus Agentic Protocol

Formal and portable body of the grammar. It declares fields, versions, compatibility, and recognizable structures.

## Nautilus Mirror Extension

Adapter and reference implementation inside Mirror Mind.

## Nautilus Harness

Dedicated application for practicing the Nautilus grammar.

## Value Generation

[Central purpose of Nautilus](value-generation.md): making meaningful value available in the Human Territory through disciplined Realization, observed Impact, and Integration.

## Human Territory

[Field where value must become available or make a difference](human-territory.md) in order for Realization to be recognized as human or human-agentic.

## Agentic Map

[Shared representation of the work context](agentic-map.md) that allows human and agentic Participants to understand where they are, what materiality exists, and what Realization is being sought in the Human Territory.

## Journey

[Field of growth](journey.md) that hosts continuity, memory, direction, and multiple [Missions](mission.md).

## Participant

[Presence that participates in the Journey](participant.md). It may have human or agentic nature.

## Nature

Fundamental characteristic of a Participant, such as human or agentic. The current version recognizes the distinction, but does not yet formalize its effects.

## Mission

Named intention of value generation through realization. In the current version, it is formulative and minimal.

## Delivery

[Produced result or effected change](delivery.md) by a Mission.

## Evidence

[Proportional trace](evidence.md) that sustains recognition that value was made available through Realization. At the high level of the Journey, it plays a role analogous to automated tests, reviews, and quality or integrity checks at lower work levels.

## Realization

[Disciplined passage through which value gains form](realization.md), crosses the Journey, touches the Human Territory, and becomes available there, sustained by Evidence.

## Impact

[What happens after a Realization becomes available](impact.md) in the Human Territory. It can be assessed through qualitative and quantitative signals, but is not controlled in the same way as Realization.

## Integration

[Gesture that preserves learning](integration.md), change, or decision in the next growth.

## Realization intelligence

[Capacity of a Journey to learn to realize better](realization-intelligence.md) at each turn of the spiral.

## Reliable continuity

[Capacity of a Journey to continue](reliable-continuity.md) without reconstructing from zero what was already lived, decided, delivered, evidenced, and learned.

## Role

Situated form of participation in a Mission or Journey. Still outside the current version.

## Authority

Legitimate capacity to decide, authorize, or recognize something inside the Journey. Still outside the current version.

## Semantic chamber

Slice of language that adds capacity to the method without breaking the previous form.

## Mission Integrity Test

Instrument for verifying whether a Mission is minimally formulated without carrying premature execution.
