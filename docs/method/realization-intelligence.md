# Realization intelligence

Realization intelligence is the capacity of a Journey to learn how to realize better at each turn of the spiral.

It is not merely cognitive intelligence, planning, productivity, or execution speed. It appears when realized work increases the capacity of the next work: more language, more criteria, more memory, more reusable evidence, and more clarity about how to transform effort into recognizable value generation.

## Why the concept exists

Humans and agents can produce many conversations, files, tasks, and decisions without increasing the Journey's capacity to produce new realizations.

This happens when work leaves dispersed traces but no integrated form. There is movement, but the next session starts almost from zero. The [Participant](participant.md) must remember what happened, reconstruct decisions, reinterpret artifacts, recover context, and rediscover what matters.

Realization intelligence names the opposite. It grows when each session leaves the next one more capable.

## How it increases

Realization intelligence increases when a Journey can:

- name the movement underway more precisely;
- recognize what was realized;
- sustain progress through evidence;
- preserve decisions and learning in reusable form;
- perceive recurring patterns;
- adjust criteria for the next Mission;
- begin the next session with more clarity than before.

## Relation to Integration

[Integration](integration.md) is the gesture that preserves learning.

Realization intelligence is the capacity that grows when integration happens well.

A [Realization](realization.md) without Integration may close a Delivery, but not necessarily increase the Journey's capacity. An integrated Realization changes the starting point of the next turn.

## Relation to agents

Agents expand speed, volume, and exploratory capacity. But this expansion only becomes realization intelligence when it produces reliable continuity.

An agent can generate many artifacts and still leave the Journey more confused. It can also produce a small synthesis, decision, or evidence that transforms the next movement. Nautilus values the second case: not output quantity, but increased capacity to realize.

## Practical signs

A Journey is increasing its realization intelligence when [Participants](participant.md) can answer more easily:

- what was realized since the last session;
- why it mattered;
- what evidence sustains progress;
- what learning was consolidated;
- what new capacity exists now;
- how this changes the next movement.

## Anti-patterns

Realization intelligence does not increase when:

- there are many artifacts, but little evidence of progress;
- there are many decisions, but no integrated memory;
- there is a lot of execution, but little clarity about what changed;
- there are many conversations, but the next encounter must reconstruct everything;
- there is agentic speed, but the Journey loses form.

## Synthesis

Realization intelligence is the cumulative gain of the spiral. Each well-integrated turn makes the Journey more capable of realizing the next one.
