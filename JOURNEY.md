# Nautilus
**Status:** active
**Stage:** Family coherence after formation

## Description

Nautilus is a family of three coordinated bodies: the Nautilus Agentic Framework, Nautilus App and Nautilus Agentic Protocol. The family preserves coherence between the conceptual framework, the practical application experience and the formal portable declaration, without collapsing their distinct responsibilities.

## Current focus

Maintain family identity, body boundaries, cross-body coherence and the public entry point. The family Journey does not own the framework corpus, app implementation or protocol specification.
